## Executive Summary

This proposal requests CCF funding to maintain Qubic marketing operations **AS IS** through August and September 2026, covering the same team structure, deliverables, tools, and salary levels as the July interim period.

Mr. Rose is acting solely as fund manager overseeing deliverables are met during this period — not as Head of Marketing. This arrangement mirrors the interim model already used for the Community Management workgroup and is explicitly transitionary.

A budget of $3,000/month has been allocated for a Marketing Coordinator role. Interviews are currently ongoing, and we expect to onboard a candidate in the coming weeks. Once in place, that person will assume day-to-day coordination responsibilities and this interim model will be retired.

> **This is not an intended long-term operating model.** The goal is continuity without interruption while the right candidate is found. Please note some marketing services and tools are fundamental for remaining relevant in the market (e.g. website, X Premium).

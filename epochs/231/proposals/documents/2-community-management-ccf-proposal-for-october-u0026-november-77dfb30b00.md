## 👥 什么是社区管理？

社区管理团队负责管理 Qubic 的 Discord 服务器和 Telegram 频道，并为多个区域性和多语言社群提供支持。社区管理作为协议开发、治理与社区参与者之间的稳定层。它还为生态系统中的其他工作组提供支持。核心团队由指定的版主和社区支持人员组成，同时也依靠志愿者。团队总人数超过20人。

**他们的职责包括：**

<br/>🤝 欢迎并引导新成员
<br/>🛡️ 执行社区规则
<br/>💬 促进建设性讨论
<br/>🎓 提供内部培训
<br/>🔧 协助其他工作组解决问题

## 🔍 过去两个月资金的使用情况 ##

由于市场状况和价格波动，社区团队采用了其动态薪酬模型，以保持在预算范围之内。尽管做出了这些调整，Discord 和 Telegram 上的运营仍持续不间断，团队全力专注于核心的版主管理、治理支持和社区安全工作。

这段时期的资金使社区管理团队得以在整个 Qubic 生态系统中维持一个安全、公平且充满活力的环境，同时也让我们得以重启一些直接惠及社区的关键举措。其中包括创建了一个专门的意见与反馈渠道，该渠道反响热烈，已经收集到许多高质量、可行动的建议。此外，在其他工作组负责人的宝贵合作与支持下，我们成功重新整理并激活了官方常见问题解答（FAQ）。尽管预算有所减少，我们目前仍在积极构思新的方式来维持社区的参与热情，同时致力于让我们的内容更容易被更广泛的受众理解和获取。

最终，除了日常的版主管理、政策执行和技术支持工作之外，我们的团队将全力致力于帮助 Qubic 在其社区内促进更深入的理解和积极的教育，以确保生态系统保持一致且信息充分。

另外需要说明的是，我们两位前任社区负责人 Tom55 和 ElClip 近期已卸下志愿者职务。除了曾经的领导工作之外，多年来他们也一直是团队众多成员的良师益友，我们在此向他们致以最诚挚而温暖的感谢，感谢他们为这个社区所带来的一切。他们的离开自然会影响我们当前的工作容量，并会体现在团队今后所能交付的工作范围上。

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 我们采用基于价格档位的**动态薪酬模型**。该模型为一个可能会调整的三级薪酬体系，根据 Qubic 的价格调整薪酬水平：若价格跌破设定阈值，薪资将相应下调；若价格回升，薪资也将恢复至更高水平。该模型使薪酬与市场表现保持一致，有助于在下行期管理预算，并在增长期给予相应奖励。此模型已在实践中得到应用，使社区团队无需进行紧急预算修订即可保持正常运作。

## 👥 What Is Community Management?

The Community Management Team moderates the Qubic Discord server and Telegram channels and supports several regional and multilingual hubs. Community Management acts as a stabilizing layer between protocol development, governance and community participants. It also brings support to other workgroups in the ecosystem. While the core team is composed of designated moderators and community support, it also relies on volunteers. Together they make up a team of more than 20 members.

**Their role include:**

<br/>🤝 Welcoming and onboarding new members
<br/>🛡️ Enforcing community rules
<br/>💬 Promoting constructive discussions
<br/>🎓 Providing internal training
<br/>🔧 Enhancing problem resolution among other workgroups

## 🔍 How We Used Past Funding during the last 2 months ##

Due to market conditions and price fluctuations, the Community Team applied its dynamic compensation model to remain within the allocated budget. Despite these adjustments, operations on Discord and Telegram continued uninterrupted, with a full focus on core moderation, governance support, and community safety.

Funding during this period enabled the Community Management Team to maintain a safe, fair, and engaging environment across the Qubic ecosystem, while allowing us to relaunch key initiatives that directly benefit the community. This included the creation of a dedicated ideas and feedback channel, which has received an excellent reception and is already yielding high-level, actionable insights. Furthermore, we have successfully reactivated the official FAQ by structuring critical information from across the ecosystem, with the valuable cooperation and support of other workgroup leads. We are also currently working on new ideas to keep the community engaged despite the reduced budget, while making our content more accessible to a wider audience.

Ultimately, beyond our day-to-day work in moderation, policy enforcement, and technical support, our team is fully committed to helping Qubic foster a deeper understanding and active education within its community, ensuring an aligned and well-informed ecosystem.

It should also be noted that two of our former Community Leads, Tom55 and ElClip, recently stepped back from their volunteer roles. Beyond their time leading the team, both have been true mentors to many of our members over the years, and we want to warmly and sincerely thank them for everything they have brought to this community. Their departure naturally reduces our current capacity and is reflected in the scope of what the team is able to deliver going forward.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> We apply a **dynamic salary model** based on price tiers. A 3-level salary system, subject to modifications, adjusts compensation based on Qubic price. If the price drops below set thresholds, salaries are reduced accordingly. If the price rises again, salaries are restored to higher levels. This model aligns compensation with market performance. It helps manage budget during downturns and rewards during growth. This model has already been applied in practice and allows the Community Team to remain operational without requiring emergency budget revisions.

### Team costs have decreased by 48% while maintaining the same operational scope.

<br/><br/>Monthly salary expenses were reduced from **$16,000** to **$11,000**; this represented approximately a **30% reduction** under current PA conditions. Several team members are taking on additional responsibilities for the same compensation, in the interest of Qubic's overall sustainability.

# Introduction

This is one of **two related proposals** being submitted together. This one funds the **Qubic Marketing Team** — the internal team within Qubic — while a second, separate proposal covers **Agency value-added services**. We are splitting them because any operating model we may ultimately define for marketing will always require a certain internal team within Qubic, and separating the two lets Computors decide on each independently: the bare-minimum internal team on its own, or the full package including the agency.

For this term we are **evolving the content approach** toward a more corporate style, and running a **reassessment of our target audience** to improve the impact of our actions. The goal is to broaden engagement across Qubic's different active roles and activities — not only investors, but all participants in the ecosystem. This is why we have **removed the entire paid media budget** and structured the team around **2 full-time equivalents (FTEs)** covering all marketing tasks. With paid media paused, our focus shifts to **organic marketing and SEO** to sustain reach and visibility.

Mr. Rose is acting solely as treasurer, managing the funds and overseeing that deliverables are met during this period — not as Head of Marketing. This arrangement mirrors the interim model already used for the Community Management workgroup and is transitionary.

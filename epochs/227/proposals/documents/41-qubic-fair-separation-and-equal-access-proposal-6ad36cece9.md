# Qubic Fair Separation and Equal Access Proposal

## Purpose

Qubic must separate private commercial interests from official authority.

No mining pool, private company, employee group, or household should be able to influence Qubic technical direction, moderation, project approval, funding, listings, or access while also benefiting commercially from those decisions.

This proposal applies equally to every entity.

## Voting Options

| Option | Outcome |
| :----: | ------- |
| **1** | Adopt mandatory separation between private commercial interests and official Qubic authority, remove official status from privately operated services, and establish equal project and technical access. |
| **0** | Continue allowing commercial affiliations and official Qubic authority to overlap under the current system. |

## 1. Mandatory Separation of Roles

Any person who is employed by, paid by, owns, advises, or receives material benefits from a private Qubic mining pool or commercial service may not simultaneously hold an official role involving:

- Discord moderation or administration
- Core-team decision-making
- Technical workgroup leadership
- Grants or incubation
- Project approval
- Listings or partnerships
- Governance boards
- Official communications
- Access to non-public technical information

Affected individuals must choose between their commercial role and their official authority.

They may continue contributing code or participating in the community as independent contributors, but they may not retain privileged decision-making or gatekeeping power.

## 2. Removal of "Official" Commercial Status

No privately owned or independently operated pool, company, product, or service may describe itself as:

- "official"
- "the official pool"
- "official Qubic services"
- any similar wording that implies exclusive endorsement

The term **official** may only be used for services directly owned, operated, and governed by Qubic under transparent community oversight.

All pools and commercial services must be listed neutrally using the same published criteria. No pool may receive privileged placement, branding, introductions, technical access, or promotion.

## 3. Independent Moderation

Employees, contractors, owners, or representatives of commercial Qubic services may not moderate official Qubic channels.

Official moderators must be independent from the businesses, pools, and projects discussed in those channels.

Moderation decisions involving projects, competition, governance criticism, or conflicts of interest must include:

- the rule that was violated
- the action taken
- the responsible moderator
- an independent appeal process

Good-faith criticism of Qubic governance or commercial conflicts must not result in retaliation, censorship, reduced project access, or exclusion.

## 4. Fair Project Access

Access to incubation, funding, listings, partnerships, technical support, and official ecosystem visibility must be based on the quality and value of a project not on personal relationships, private introductions, Discord connections, or affiliation with an existing commercial entity.

All projects must receive equal consideration under the same standards. Projects that compete with an established pool, company, or service must not be disadvantaged, delayed, ignored, or denied access because of that competition.

Anyone with a financial, professional, or household interest in a project or competing service must not influence its evaluation, visibility, funding, or access to Qubic contributors.

No moderator, developer, board member, mining pool, or commercial entity may privately block a legitimate project, restrict its opportunity to be heard, or retaliate against its creators for raising concerns about conflicts of interest.

Every builder must have a fair opportunity to contribute to Qubic, regardless of whom they know or whom their project may compete with.

## 5. Equal Technical Information

Protocol updates, APIs, mining changes, algorithm specifications, integration requirements, and other commercially valuable technical information must be released simultaneously to all qualified participants.

No private pool or company may receive advance information because its employees or associates also hold Qubic roles.

Security-sensitive information may remain private only for the minimum time necessary to address an active vulnerability.

## 6. Household and Related-Party Conflicts

Spouses, partners, relatives, or members of the same household may contribute to Qubic, but they may not:

- supervise each other
- approve each other's compensation
- appoint each other to official roles
- review projects affecting a household-linked business
- jointly control the same approval process

Relevant relationships must be disclosed, and one person must leave the affected decision chain.

## 7. Implementation

| Timeline | Required Action |
| -------- | --------------- |
| **Within 7 days of approval** | Qubic must publish all covered official roles and relevant commercial affiliations. |
| **Within 14 days** | Conflicted individuals must choose between their commercial position and their official authority. Until that choice is completed, they must be suspended from moderation, funding, project approval, listings, governance, and privileged technical decisions. |
| **Within 30 days** | Entity must remove misleading official commercial branding, establish independent moderation, and review decisions made under undisclosed conflicts. |

Violations may result in removal from the relevant official role and an independent re-review of affected decisions.

---

## Closing Statement

This proposal does not accuse any person or company of corruption.

It recognises that fairness cannot exist where the same network can hold commercial interests, moderate criticism, influence technical decisions, approve projects, and control access to the core team.

No private company should possess official authority over its competitors. No builder should need insider relationships to receive a fair hearing.

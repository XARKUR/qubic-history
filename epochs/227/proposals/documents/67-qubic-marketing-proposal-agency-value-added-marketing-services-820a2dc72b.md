## SEO Diagnosis: qubic.org

Cointribune has already run a live SEO audit of qubic.org through its Semrush stack and delivered it as a 12-page professional report ([link](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TbV2xiSFhxd5sLX0kvSklFcyZ0yYf1wI/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=112352700743020354330&rtpof=true&sd=true)). The diagnosis was clear and urgent:

- **Dangerous keyword concentration** — roughly 40% of the site's organic traffic depends on a single technical term ("qatum"). Any ranking loss there collapses overall visibility.
- **A shrinking footprint** — keyword coverage has fallen by about 60% over recent months.
- **Almost no non-brand capture** — qubic.org barely ranks for the generic queries new users search (education, comparisons, use-cases), capturing almost none of the top-of-funnel audience.
- **A toxic backlink profile** — 35,057 links from a single domain, with spam anchors and PBN signals, dragging the Authority Score (38 → 32 since January) down despite growing link volume.

The remediation roadmap Cointribune executes as part of this engagement: diagnosis (delivered), reputation clean-up (backlink audit and disavow of the toxic profile, ~50k links), discovery content base (the non-brand content that captures top-of-funnel search, where the 12 articles plug in), structural diversification (broadening the keyword and page footprint), and ongoing stabilization (monitoring, position tracking, monthly reporting).

**N.B.** Given the urgency, making qubic.org fully SEO-friendly is a large undertaking that will take more than three months. Qubic and Cointribune news will be used to offset the drop in authority score at minimal cost in the interim. To achieve results faster, a larger media-coverage budget would be advisable; a one-year roadmap can be developed using Qubic's Google Analytics.

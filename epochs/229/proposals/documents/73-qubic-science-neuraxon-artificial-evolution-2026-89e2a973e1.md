## Rationale

The standard pipeline for scientific research runs: paper → tests → proofs → submission to a peer-reviewed conference → if accepted, revision in light of reviewer comments → registration and publication fees → presentation at the conference.

**If a paper is not presented, it is not published.** Acceptance is the first milestone; registration and presentation are the ones that convert months of work into a permanent, citable record. Without them, a substantial part of the effort is wasted.

A peer-reviewed article is the high-quality standard in science. For a project of Qubic's ambition, an accumulating body of externally reviewed, independently audited research demonstrates credibility to prospective investors, partners and institutional collaborators. Qubic stands on its own merits, but the scientific layer helps spread, validate and reinforce CFB's vision.

Very few company-based AI and crypto projects carry a genuine scientific track record. Four accepted peer-reviewed papers in a single year — at venues in Japan, Germany, the United States and now France — is a distinctive and defensible asset, and one that compounds: published papers are cited, and citations bring further collaboration.

The work continues. Multi-Neuraxon is released, Neuraxon-Live has just shipped, and Qiner integration will follow once the Ant Colony is deployed and tested; those will be the subject of separate proposals. 

This is the fourth paper, and it will not be the last.
